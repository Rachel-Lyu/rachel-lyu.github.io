About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the School of Computer Science at Carnegie Mellon University, advised by Roni Rosenfeld and Bryan Wilder. I work on machine learning for epidemiology and public health.

My interests also include interpretable machine learning, distribution shift, statistical inference, causal discovery, and graph learning.
